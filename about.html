Developingyoung minds

Quest Breed Schools is a creche, nursery and primary school at Mayfair Gardens on the Lekki-Epe Expressway. Our promise is simple: to help every child learn, create and thrive.

Who we are

A school that feels like a garden

We care for children from the creche through to the end of primary school, in one bright campus in Ibeju-Lekki.

Our days are hands-on. Nursery pupils count on number boards their teachers make, finger paint, sing and recite. Primary pupils read, reason, use computers and play chess, and they have represented the school at a chess tournament.

We celebrate together too. Picnic Day by the pool, Children’s Day games, the Father’s Day cook-off and our graduation and prize-giving ceremony bring families onto the campus through the year.

Our crest

What our crest stands for

  • An open book

    Learning sits at the heart of everything we do, and every page is open to every child.

  • Pages of many colours

    No two children are alike. Each one grows in their own colour and at their own pace.

  • A child reaching up

    Rising out of the book, arms open, ready for whatever comes next.

  • Petals in bloom

    Curiosity, opening one petal at a time. It is why we say: where curiosity blooms.

Words on our walls

The values on our walls

These words are painted and posted around our campus. They are the values we teach, and the ones we try to live by.

What we want every child to believe

I am worthy. I am kind.

For the International Day of the Boy Child, our boys held up the words we hope every child at Quest Breed carries with them.

Leaders, caregivers, friends and advocates for change.
