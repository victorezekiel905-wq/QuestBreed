Quest Breed Schools is a creche, nursery and primary school at Mayfair Gardens, Km 37 on the Lekki-Epe Expressway. Our promise is to help every child learn, create and thrive.

Creche · Nursery · Primary

Our name

Two words, one promise.

Our name describes the school we set out to be, and the journey we take with every child.

Quest

A search for something worth finding

Every child arrives full of questions. We want them to keep asking, to enjoy looking for answers, and to discover what they are good at along the way.

Breed

To raise, nurture and grow

We raise children with patience and care, the way a gardener tends a garden: knowing that every child blooms, each in their own colour and their own time.

Our promise

Developing young minds to learn, create and thrive.

Our motto

Where curiosity blooms: an open book, and a child rising from its pages with ideas blooming all around.

Our values

The words on our walls.

Painted and posted around our campus, and taught every day.

  • Every child is gifted

    They just unwrap their packages at different times. We notice each child’s gifts and help them grow.

  • Do the right thing

    Even when no one is looking. Honesty and good manners are taught, modelled and praised.

  • We inspire

    Bright classrooms, new activities and celebrations that make children want to learn more.

  • We motivate

    Every small win is noticed and cheered, from a matched number to a poem said with confidence.

  • We care

    Warm, attentive adults who know the children and look after them like family.

  • We teach

    Strong foundations in reading, writing and numbers, taught hands-on so children truly understand.

Our approach

Learn, create and thrive.

Three words from our promise, and three things every day at Quest Breed is designed to grow, together.

Learn

Minds that understand

Numbers, letter sounds and reading taught with real things to hold, so children understand, not just remember.

Create

Hands that make

Painting, colouring, building and performing give every child a way to share their ideas.

Thrive

Hearts that grow

Kindness, confidence and good character, celebrated with families all through the year.

Our teachers

Teachers who sit beside you.

In our classrooms you will find teachers right next to the children at the number board, cheering every match and every new word.

Our teachers and caregivers are warm, patient and easy to talk to. They use hands-on materials so learning makes sense, and they celebrate the small wins with each child.

Join our team

Painted on our wall

“Do the right thing, even when no one is looking.”

One of the first things visitors read in our courtyard, and a rule our pupils are proud to keep.
