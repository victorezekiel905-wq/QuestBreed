Learning youcan touch

Our children learn with their hands, their voices and their imaginations. Here is how each stage works, from the creche to primary school.

Creche

A gentle first step

Our creche gives babies and toddlers a calm, caring start, with steady routines and lots of reassurance for the youngest members of our school family.

  • Calm daily routines with time to rest and play
  • Sensory play, songs and first words
  • Soft, safe play spaces indoors and out
Pre-Nursery

Colours, shapes and stories

Pre-nursery is where little hands discover what they can make. Every day has something to paint, sing, build or share.

  • Finger painting and early mark-making
  • Songs, rhymes and story time
  • Learning to share, take turns and say hello
Nursery

Counting, sounding, building

In nursery, numbers and letters become things you can hold. Our teachers make hands-on materials like number boards so children learn by doing.

  • Number boards for counting and matching
  • Letter sounds and early reading
  • Reciting and speaking in front of the class
Primary

Confident, curious pupils

Primary pupils build strong foundations in reading, writing and mathematics, and the confidence to stand up and speak, perform and compete.

  • Reading, writing and mathematics
  • Computer lessons
  • Chess, including tournaments with other schools
  • Poems, speeches and performances at school events

How children learn here

Six ways a day comes alive

Each of these comes from a real day at Quest Breed, filmed or photographed on campus.

Learning with our hands

Number boards, matching games and threading turn counting into something you can touch.

Art and colour

Finger painting and colouring build the small-muscle control that writing needs later.

Speaking with confidence

Pupils recite poems and speak in front of classmates and families at school events.

Chess

Planning ahead, keeping calm and shaking hands at the end, win or lose.

Water play

The paddling pool comes out on special days like Picnic Day, always with adults close by.

Families in the classroom

Days like the Father’s Day cook-off bring parents in to cook, sing and celebrate with us.

Weekly facts

Did you know?

Every week we share a fun fact with our pupils and families on our social pages. Here are a few favourites.

Elephants are the only animals that can’t jump.

You blink about 20 times a minute. That’s over 28,000 times a day!

Rainbows are actually full circles, but we usually only see half of one.

A snail can sleep for up to three years at a time if conditions aren’t right.
