Learningyou can touch,every day.

Each stage builds on the one before, so children move up with confidence. Here is what learning looks like in our creche, pre-nursery, nursery and primary classes.

Hands-on, every day

01 · Babies & toddlers

Creche.

A calm, caring first step away from home.

Our creche keeps days calm and unhurried, with time to rest, play, sing and explore, and familiar faces close by.

  • Calm daily routines for rest and play
  • Sensory play, songs and rhymes
  • First words and picture books
  • Soft, safe spaces indoors and out
Enquire about Creche

02 · Little learners

Pre-Nursery.

Colours, shapes and stories.

Little hands learn to hold a crayon, dip a finger in paint, share a toy and say hello, with something new to make every day.

  • Finger painting and mark-making
  • Songs, rhymes and story time
  • Shapes, colours and first counting
  • Sharing and taking turns
Enquire about Pre-Nursery

03 · Early years

Nursery.

Where numbers become things you can hold.

Nursery children learn by doing: threading number boards, matching shapes, sounding out letters and reciting rhymes for their classmates.

  • Number boards for counting and matching
  • Letter sounds and early reading
  • Art, colour and finger painting
  • Reciting in front of the class
Enquire about Nursery

04 · Primary years

Primary.

Strong foundations, confident voices.

Primary pupils build secure skills in reading, writing and mathematics, learn to use computers, and take part in chess, poems and performances at school events.

  • English and Mathematics
  • Computer lessons
  • Chess, including tournaments
  • Poems, speeches and performances
Enquire about Primary

How we teach

Four habits in every classroom.

Whatever the age or subject, these are the principles you will see at work at Quest Breed.

  • Learn by doing

    Real things to hold, thread, paint and build, so ideas make sense.

  • Ask why

    Questions are welcome. Curiosity is where learning starts.

  • Speak up

    Reciting, presenting and performing build confident voices.

  • Celebrate small wins

    Every new word and every right answer is noticed and cheered.
