How to join

Four simple steps

There is no rush and no question too small. We will guide you through every step.

  1. Say hello

    Call or WhatsApp 0802 441 2737 or 0707 676 3237, or send the enquiry form below.

  2. Visit the school

    Come and see the classrooms and turf, and meet the teachers. Bring your child along.

  3. Register

    We will take you through registration, fees, uniform and everything else you need to know.

  4. First day

    We help your child settle in, and keep you close as they find their feet.

Questions

Things parents often ask

Which classes are you admitting into?

Admission is in progress for Creche, Pre-Nursery, Nursery and Primary. Call or WhatsApp us to check space in your child’s class.

Where is the school?

We are at Km 37, Lekki-Epe Expressway, opposite Enyo Filling Station, in Mayfair Gardens, Ibeju, Lagos. Open in Google Maps

Can we visit before applying?

Yes, please do. Call or WhatsApp us to arrange a time, and bring your child along to see the classrooms.

How much are the school fees?

Fees depend on the class. Call or WhatsApp us on 0802 441 2737 or 0707 676 3237 and we will share the current fees with you.

How can I see what happens at school?

Follow us on Instagram and Facebook, where we share our celebrations, events and weekly facts.
