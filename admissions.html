Which classes are you admitting into?

Admission is in progress for Creche, Pre-Nursery, Nursery and Primary. Call or WhatsApp us to check space in your child’s class.

Can we visit before applying?

Yes, please do. Call or WhatsApp us on 0802 441 2737 or 0707 676 3237 to arrange a time, and bring your child along.

How do I find out about fees?

Fees depend on the class. Ask us by phone or WhatsApp, or during your visit, and we will talk you through them.
