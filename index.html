Welcome to Quest Breed

About Quest Breed Schools

Quest Breed Schools is a creche, nursery and primary school in Ibeju, Lagos. Like the open book on our crest, we help every child learn, create and thrive, one bright page at a time.

Our classes

Four stages, one curious child

From first cuddles in the creche to confident pupils in primary uniform, every stage builds on the one before.

Creche

Calm, caring days for our youngest: steady routines, rest, sensory play and first words.

Creche

Pre-Nursery

Colours, shapes, songs and stories. Little hands learn to hold a crayon, share a toy and say hello.

Pre-Nursery

Nursery

Counting boards, letter sounds, painting and rhymes. Learning by touching, building and talking.

Nursery

Primary

Pupils who read, reason, recite and play chess, growing in confidence every term.
