Welcome to Quest Breed

A bright school where curiosity blooms.

At Quest Breed Schools, children from the creche to primary are encouraged to ask questions, try new things and grow in confidence, in the classroom, on the turf and at every celebration.

Our crest says it best: an open book, with a child rising from its pages and ideas blooming all around. It is how we see every child who comes through our gate, ready to learn, create and thrive.

Discover our story
Every child is gifted.

Our classes

Four classes, one curious journey.

Children can join us as babies and grow with us all the way through primary school, with each stage building on the one before.

Babies & toddlers

Creche

Gentle, loving care with songs, stories, sensory play and calm routines for rest and play.
Little learners

Pre-Nursery

Colours, shapes, songs and stories, and the first steps in holding a crayon and sharing.
Early years

Nursery

Learning through play: number boards, letter sounds, finger painting, rhymes and recitation.
Primary years

Primary

Reading, writing and mathematics, with computer lessons, chess and the confidence to speak up.

Why families choose us

The Quest Breed difference.

Six things you will notice from the moment you walk through our gate.

  • Curiosity first

    Children are encouraged to ask why, try things out and learn by doing.

  • Hands-on learning

    Number boards, counting games and finger painting turn ideas into things children can touch.

  • Kindness and character

    Our walls remind every pupil to do the right thing, even when no one is looking.

  • Chess and computers

    Pupils learn computers and chess, and have represented the school at a chess tournament.

  • Confident voices

    Poems, recitations and performances help children speak up in front of classmates and families.

  • Families as partners

    Picnic Day, the Father’s Day cook-off and graduation bring families onto our campus.
